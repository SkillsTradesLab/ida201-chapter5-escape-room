IDA201 MOTORS & CONTROLS — CHAPTER 5 ESCAPE ROOM — TEACHER GUIDE

WHAT YOU GET
- 5 themed rooms
- 25 total questions (5 per room)
- Every question starts as short answer.
- If the first response is incorrect, that same question automatically becomes multiple choice.
- Each room includes a visual clue/background.
- Final 5-digit escape code: 47295
- First-try score and elapsed time are shown at the end.

TOPICS
Room 1 — Loads: loads, motors, solenoids, heaters, signal lights
Room 2 — Relays & Contactors: contactors, relays, energized/de-energized, NO contacts, poles
Room 3 — Switches: disconnects, push-buttons, poles, heating/cooling thermostats
Room 4 — Safety & Control: fuses, thermal/magnetic overloads, magnetic starters, 24-V control circuits
Room 5 — Wiring Diagrams: schematic, pictorial, factual, installation, L1/L2

CLASSROOM USE
Students can work individually, in pairs, or in teams. For a competition, rank students/teams by First-Try Keys and then fastest completion time.

ANSWER KEY
Room 1: Loads; Motor; Solenoid; Heater; Signal light
Room 2: Contactor; Relay; De-energized; Close; One set of contacts
Room 3: Disconnect switch; Push-button switch; One; Closes; Opens
Room 4: Fuse; Heat; Magnetism; Overload protection; 24 volts
Room 5: Schematic diagram; Pictorial diagram; Factual diagram; Installation diagram; L1 and L2
